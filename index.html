How to play
  1. You get a German verb. Give everything about it: what it means, and the er/sie/es form in Präsens, Präteritum and Perfekt.
Example
geben
Meaningdonner or to give
Präsenser gibt
Präteritumer gab
Perfekter hat gegeben
  1. Press Check (or Enter). Green is right. Red is wrong, and the right answer appears under it.
  2. Every right answer scores points. Get 5 right in a row and your points double. Keep going for ×3 and ×4.
  3. Pick a game below and press Start. The full test is already set up, so the first time you can just press Start.
  • You don't need to type er. Just the verb: gibt.
  • Perfekt always needs hat or ist: hat gegeben, ist gegangen.
  • The meaning can be in French or in English.
  • No umlaut key? Use the ä ö ü buttons, or type ae, oe, ue. Both ss and ß work.
